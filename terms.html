Plain language

Terms and disclaimer

CaseShield helps you organize what to watch and verify during an immigrant visa disruption. It does not tell you what your case will do, and it is not a substitute for qualified advice.

Not legal advice

CaseShield provides information, monitoring structure and official-source context. It does not provide individualized legal advice, does not create an attorney–client relationship, and is not a law firm. Where your situation raises a legal question, the product's role is to tell you that a question exists — not to answer it.

No government affiliation

CaseShield is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of the U.S. Department of State, USCIS, the National Visa Center, or any embassy or consulate.

No outcome prediction

Nothing in CaseShield predicts whether a visa will be issued, when administrative processing will conclude, or when an appointment will be rescheduled. Monitoring plans describe what to watch and where to verify it. They are not forecasts.

Verify against official instructions

Embassy instructions change, and they differ between posts. Any plan the product produces should be checked against the written instructions you received and the official channels for your case. Where they conflict, the official instruction governs.

Validation beta

This site is a validation test. Illustrative figures are labelled as such and are not live data feeds. The $29 action measures willingness to pay: no payment is processed, no card details are collected, and no account is created. Features shown as previews may never be built.

Availability

The service is provided as-is during this beta, without warranty, and may change or be withdrawn at any time.
