EVIDENCE STANDARD

A report is never presented as an official fact.

CaseShield’s product direction is built around source type, corroboration and freshness — so a useful signal does not become a misleading claim.

01 · Highest weight

Official notice

USCIS, NVC, Department of State, an embassy or an authorized appointment system.

Label: Official · verified
02 · Direct context

Operational change

A case portal, appointment page or written instruction that affects the applicant’s next check.

Label: Direct · verify scope
03 · Supporting context

Community report

An anonymous report that can show a pattern but cannot confirm another person’s case.

Label: Community · unverified
