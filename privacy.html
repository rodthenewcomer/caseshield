Plain language

Privacy

CaseShield is in validation beta. It is an information and monitoring product, not a case-management system, and it is built so that it cannot hold your immigration identifiers even if someone asked it to.

What CaseShield collects

  • An anonymous session ID. A random value generated in your browser. It carries no identity and exists only so that one visitor reloading a page is not counted as several people.
  • Your assessment answers. The disruption type, visa category, embassy or city, where you are in the process, and what matters most to you right now.
  • Product-interest events. Which steps you reached and whether you clicked the alert or $29 buttons.
  • Campaign attribution. If you arrived from an ad or a link, the campaign parameters in the address and the host name of the site you came from — for example google.com.

What CaseShield never collects

  • A-number
  • USCIS receipt number
  • NVC case number
  • Passport number
  • Social Security number
  • Date of birth
  • Immigration documents or uploads
  • Your name or email address
  • Card or payment details

If something that looks like a case number is typed into the embassy field, your browser refuses it before anything is sent, and the server redacts it again if it arrives by any other route.

About your IP address

Your network address is used only to count requests per minute so the site cannot be flooded. That counter expires within two minutes and your address is never written into an analytics record, never stored alongside your answers, and never used for attribution or profiling.

The full address of the page you came from

Only the host name is kept. The rest of a referring address is discarded, because a search result address can contain the words you typed into a search engine, and those are not ours to hold.

Where the data lives, and for how long

  • Analytics are stored with Upstash, a managed Redis provider, and the site is hosted on Vercel.
  • Aggregate totals — how many unique sessions reached each step — are kept for as long as the validation test runs.
  • Day-by-day breakdowns are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • A rolling sample of the most recent 500 events is kept for diagnostics. Older events fall out of it automatically.

No payment is taken

The $29 action measures whether the product is worth paying for. No charge is made, no card is collected, and no checkout exists.

What CaseShield is not

CaseShield is an independent product. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any U.S. government agency. It is not a law firm and does not provide individualized legal advice, and it does not predict whether a case will be approved.

Questions or a security report

Security issues can be reported through a private GitHub security advisory on the project repository. Please do not include real immigration identifiers or documents in a report.
